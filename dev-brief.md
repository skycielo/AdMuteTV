## 🎯 Project Purpose

AdMuteTV is a lightweight Android TV app that **automatically mutes commercials** during streaming or live TV. It helps users avoid unwanted audio from ads and restores sound once the show resumes — **no remote required**.

This app is designed to be affordable, device-friendly, and platform-agnostic in its long-term roadmap.
